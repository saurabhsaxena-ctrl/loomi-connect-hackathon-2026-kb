# Closing Ceremony Playbook

## Format

The closing ceremony was run as a live webinar via LUMA. Participants attended as audience members; top teams and speakers could be promoted to panellist status during the live event.

## Invite List

Built by combining two sources:
1. LUMA event guest list (registrants who signed up for the closing ceremony event)
2. Slack workspace member list (all hackathon participants)

Total invited: ~83 participants. Always merge both lists — coverage gaps exist if you rely on only one source.

## Pre-Ceremony Prep

### Top Team DMs (send 24-48 hrs before)
Send personalised Slack DMs to top 3-4 teams. Message should include:
- Congratulations (without revealing exact rank)
- Request to be available for live ceremony
- What to expect (brief demo, Q&A, award)
- Any prep they need to do

### Slide Deck
Prepare a board-ready deck covering:
- Opening quote / hook
- Hackathon stats (teams, countries, submissions)
- What was built (category breakdown)
- Winner announcements with one-liners
- Key insights and what comes next

### Stats to Have Ready
- Total registrations: 102
- Countries: 29
- Final submissions: 25
- Tracks: 6
- Judges: 25+
- MCP tools available: 89

## Ceremony Flow

1. Opening — energy setter, hackathon story in 2 minutes
2. Stats slide — the numbers that surprise
3. What was built — category highlights
4. Winner announcements — top 4 with project one-liners
5. Winner demos or video clips (if available)
6. Insights — what the hackathon revealed
7. What comes next — community, next edition, follow-ups
8. Close

## Post-Ceremony

- Share recording with all participants
- Publish winner announcement on relevant channels
- Follow up with top teams for case study / co-marketing conversations
- Archive all slides and recordings in the knowledge base
