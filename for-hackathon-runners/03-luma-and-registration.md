# LUMA and Registration

## How Registration Worked

Registration for the hackathon was handled through **LUMA** (lu.ma) — the event platform used for managing sign-ups, event pages, and attendee communication.

## LUMA Setup

- Created a dedicated hackathon event page on LUMA
- Participants registered directly on LUMA
- LUMA captured basic registrant information
- From LUMA, participants were directed to the Netlify onboarding app

## The LUMA to Netlify Handoff

After registering on LUMA, participants received onboarding instructions directing them to **hackathon.loomi.ai** — the custom Netlify app where they completed full team registration, received their welcome kit, and joined Slack.

LUMA handled the event management layer. The Netlify app handled the hackathon-specific onboarding.

## Closing Ceremony

LUMA was also used for the **closing ceremony** event. The invite list was built by combining:
- LUMA event guests
- Slack workspace members

Combined list covered ~83 participants invited to the closing ceremony.

## What Worked

- LUMA is clean, professional, and familiar to most participants
- The two-step flow (LUMA registration → Netlify onboarding) worked well
- LUMA handled reminders and event comms automatically

## What to Watch

- Keep the LUMA to Netlify redirect clear and immediate in confirmation emails
- Ensure both lists (LUMA + Slack) are merged before closing ceremony invites go out
