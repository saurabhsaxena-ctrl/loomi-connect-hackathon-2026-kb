# Slack Workspace Setup

## Purpose

The hackathon Slack workspace was the primary communication layer for participants during the build phase. It served as support hub, community space, and operational backbone.

## Channel Structure

| Channel | Purpose |
|---|---|
| #announcements | Official updates, deadline reminders, winner announcements |
| #general | Community discussion, team formation, general questions |
| #loomi-connect | Loomi Connect MCP technical help and sandbox support |
| #track-1 through #track-6 | Track-specific discussion and Q&A |
| #submissions | Submission guidance and deadline reminders |
| #demo-day | Closing ceremony info and links |

## Onboarding Flow

Participants joined Slack through the Netlify onboarding app (hackathon.loomi.ai). After completing team registration, they were directed to join the workspace. This created a clean link between registration data and Slack membership.

## Key Operational Use

- Judge assignment notifications sent via Slack DM
- Top 3 teams notified of closing ceremony via personalised Slack DMs
- Support questions answered in #loomi-connect
- Deadline reminders broadcast in #announcements

## Closing Ceremony Invites

For the closing ceremony, the Slack workspace member list was combined with the LUMA event guest list to build the full invite list (~83 participants). Always merge both lists — some participants may be in one but not the other.

## Lesson Learned

Slack was more effective than email for mid-event communication. Participants were already active in the workspace, so announcements had near-instant read rates. Use Slack as the primary channel and email as backup only.
