# Concept and Planning

## What Was the Hackathon?

The Loomi Connect AI Hackathon 2026 was Bloomreach's first external AI hackathon. The goal was to open Loomi Connect MCP to the world and see what customers, partners, and developers would build on it.

## The Core Bet

> "We gave the world our AI platform and said: build something remarkable."

The hackathon served two purposes:
1. **External validation** — do customers believe in Loomi Connect enough to build on it?
2. **Product intelligence** — what use cases does the market naturally reach for?

## Format Decisions

| Decision | Choice | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Internal vs External | External | Needed market signal, not internal demos |
| Theme | Build an AI agent on Loomi Connect MCP | Specific enough to focus, open enough to surprise |
| Tracks | 6 challenge tracks | Guided creativity without constraining it |
| Judging | 4 judges per team, 5 criteria | Independent scoring prevents bias |
| Prizes | Top 4 teams recognised | Recognition over cash — community signal |

## The 6 Challenge Tracks

1. **Track 1** — Commerce and Merchandising Agents
2. **Track 2** — Campaign and Personalisation Agents
3. **Track 3** — Analytics Agents and Decision Intelligence
4. **Track 4** — Customer Lifecycle and Retention Agents
5. **Track 5** — Autonomous Campaign and Content Agents
6. **Track 6** — Cross-MCP Orchestration (Advanced)

Track 6 was the advanced track — teams had to orchestrate across multiple MCP servers. It attracted the strongest submissions.

## Key Insight from Planning

Almost every strong submission came from teams who built for **marketers and CRM teams**, not for shoppers. The shopper-facing agent space was almost entirely untouched — a clear market whitespace signal.
