# Tracks and Judging Criteria

## The 6 Challenge Tracks

| Track | Name | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Track 1 | Commerce and Merchandising Agents | Shopper-facing discovery, search, recommendations |
| Track 2 | Campaign and Personalisation Agents | 1:1 personalisation, campaign intelligence |
| Track 3 | Analytics Agents and Decision Intelligence | Insight generation, CRM decision support |
| Track 4 | Customer Lifecycle and Retention Agents | Churn prevention, re-engagement, lifecycle |
| Track 5 | Autonomous Campaign and Content Agents | Content generation, campaign automation |
| Track 6 | Cross-MCP Orchestration (Advanced) | Multi-MCP agents, complex orchestration |

Track 6 was the advanced track requiring teams to use multiple Loomi Connect MCP servers together. It produced the strongest submissions — all 4 top teams submitted under Track 6.

## Track Submission Distribution (25 final submissions)

- Track 6 (Cross-MCP): 9 teams — most popular and highest scoring
- Track 4 (Lifecycle and Retention): 5 teams
- Track 1 (Commerce): 4 teams
- Track 3 (Analytics): 3 teams
- Track 2 (Campaign): 3 teams
- Track 5 (Content): 1 team

## Judging Criteria

Each criterion scored 0-5. Maximum total: 25 points.

### 1. Innovation and Creativity (0-5)
Does the idea feel fresh and original? Did the team find a clever or unexpected way to use Loomi Connect MCP? Does it go beyond the obvious use case?

- 0 = Generic, no originality
- 3 = Solid idea, expected approach
- 5 = Genuinely creative — unexpected and impressive

### 2. Technical Execution (0-5)
Does the agent actually work? Is the integration with Loomi Connect MCP stable? Is the code well-structured? Does the demo show a working end-to-end solution?

- 0 = Broken or did not run
- 3 = Works with some rough edges
- 5 = Polished, reliable, production-ready feel

### 3. Business Value and Impact (0-5)
Would a real Bloomreach customer use this? Does it solve a meaningful real-world problem? Could this drive measurable outcomes?

- 0 = No clear business case
- 3 = Solves a real problem, value is clear
- 5 = High-impact — would make a customer's business measurably better

### 4. MCP Usage Quality (0-5)
How deeply and thoughtfully did the team use Loomi Connect MCP? Are MCP calls central to the solution or just a checkbox integration?

- 0 = MCP barely used or irrelevant
- 3 = MCP is used meaningfully as part of the solution
- 5 = MCP is the backbone of the agent — deep, creative, multi-capability usage

### 5. Demo and Presentation (0-5)
Was the demo clear and compelling? Did the team communicate the problem, solution, and value effectively? Would an exec or customer watching be impressed?

- 0 = Unclear, confusing, or demo failed
- 3 = Clear and covered the basics well
- 5 = Polished, engaging, and memorable

## Judging Process

- 4 judges assigned per team (columns Judge 1-4 in the submission sheet)
- Judges scored independently — no calibration session before scoring
- Scores averaged across judges for final ranking
- Judge notes kept internal; written feedback shared with teams post-event
