## What Was Built

25 teams submitted working agents across 6 tracks. The dominant use case was marketing intelligence and CRM automation. Notable builds:
- Autonomous campaign agents that take a plain-language goal and build a full Bloomreach campaign
- CX risk officers that monitor churn signals across all three MCP servers
- CRM planning agents that act as a senior strategist
- Discount suppression agents that protect margins at product launch

## The Sharpest Insight

Almost every team built for marketers, not shoppers. Teams had access to 89 MCP tools and the most common use was helping CRM teams decide what to do next — not helping end customers shop or discover products. The shopper-facing agent space is a clear whitespace.
