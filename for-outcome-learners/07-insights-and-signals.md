# Insights and Signals

> What the Loomi Connect AI Hackathon 2026 revealed about the market, the platform, and the opportunity.

---

## Signal 1 — The Market Built for Marketers, Not Shoppers

Almost every team built an agent for **CRM managers, marketing ops leads, and lifecycle marketers** — not for end shoppers. Teams had access to 89 MCP tools including commerce and discovery capabilities, yet the overwhelming instinct was to help the marketer do their job better.

Only 2-3 teams genuinely targeted the end shopper (Agentic Flow, Terra, Springbok Agency). The rest built internal decision intelligence tools.

**What this means:** The shopper-facing agent opportunity is entirely untapped. No one has built the definitive AI shopping agent on Loomi Connect yet.

---

## Signal 2 — MCP Adoption Is Real, Not Theoretical

Teams didn't struggle to find use cases for Loomi Connect. The hardest part was choosing which problem to solve first. Every team that submitted had a backlog of ideas — they only built one because of time constraints.

This validates that customers have been waiting for an integration layer like Loomi Connect. The demand pre-existed the product.

---

## Signal 3 — Track 6 Won Everything

All 4 top teams competed in Track 6 (Cross-MCP Orchestration). The teams that used multiple MCP servers together produced dramatically better outcomes than single-MCP teams.

This has product implications: agents that orchestrate across Marketing + Analytics + Conversations are more valuable than agents that use one MCP in isolation. The value multiplies with breadth.

---

## Signal 4 — Customers Build for Revenue, Not Demos

Top teams built agents tied to measurable business outcomes:
- Cart recovery and churn prevention (Rohlik, IbxLab)
- Margin protection at launch (TV Nova)
- CRM efficiency (LoomiTown, Vielendark)

None of the top submissions were "cool demos." Every winner solved something a business would pay to fix.

---

## Signal 5 — Partners See Commercial Opportunity

Agency and SI partners (Bounteous, Ansira, Springbok, IM Digital) built client-ready prototypes. When your partners spend sprint time building on your platform, that is ecosystem pull — they are investing because they see revenue opportunity.

---

## Signal 6 — 29 Countries Without a Sales Team

Teams joined from countries where Bloomreach has no direct sales coverage. Organic developer community reach is already happening. A lightweight developer community (Slack, docs, office hours) would compound this.

---

## The One-Line Board Summary

> "83 teams had access to the same platform and 89 tools — and almost all of them built for the marketer, not the shopper. The shopper-side agent opportunity is still wide open."
