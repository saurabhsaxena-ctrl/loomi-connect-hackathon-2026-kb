## 🥇 LoomiTown — SilentWatch
**Score: 23.5 / 25 | Track 6 — Cross-MCP Orchestration**
**Company:** Investown Technologies, Czech Republic
**Lead:** Martina Nemcova (martina.nemcova@investown.cz)

### One-liner
Diagnoses why your customers went silent, automatically.

### What They Built
A weekly autonomous agent that identifies non-responding customers, groups them into behavioural cohorts, and generates an analytical report with hypotheses about the causes of inactivity — without prescribing direct actions. The value is diagnosis: saving CRM managers hours of manual analysis and providing a solid foundation for their own decisions.

### Why It Won
Perfect scores in Technical Execution (5.0) and MCP Usage Quality (5.0). The team understood that the value isn't automation — it's giving humans better information to make decisions. The agent doesn't act, it explains.

## 🥈 Rohlik Team — Orbit
**Score: 22.5 / 25 | Track 6 — Cross-MCP Orchestration**
**Company:** Rohlik Group, Czech Republic
**Lead:** Danila Kossygin (danila.kossygin@rohlik.cz)

### One-liner
Your marketing goal to live campaign, no manual work.

### What They Built
A closed-loop campaign agent for lean marketing teams. Takes a free-text marketing goal, discovers a real customer cohort in Bloomreach, builds a full campaign, monitors performance, and proposes iterations — with humans approving at each step but not executing.

### Why It Scored High
Highest Business Value score (4.8). The use case is universally relatable: small marketing teams doing too much manually. Orbit is a force multiplier, not a replacement.

## 🥈 Team IbxLab — Lighthouse
**Score: 22.5 / 25 | Track 6 — Cross-MCP Orchestration**
**Company:** IbxLab
**Lead:** iabdulkarim (iabdulkarim.eth@ibxlab.com)

### One-liner
Catches churn risk before it becomes lost revenue.

### What They Built
An autonomous CX Risk Officer that orchestrates across all three Loomi Connect MCPs simultaneously — Marketing MCP for journey overlap and audience fatigue, Analytics MCP for engagement decay and cohort drift, Conversations MCP for friction signals and sentiment. Detects compounding risk before it materialises as churn and prepares human-reviewable intervention briefs.

### Why It Scored High
Highest Innovation (4.8) and Business Value (5.0). The circuit-breaker risk scoring architecture was technically impressive. The only team to deeply and creatively use all three MCPs together.

## 🏅 Vielendark — CRM Pathfinder
**Score: 22.3 / 25 | Track 6 — Cross-MCP Orchestration**
**Company:** Vielendark
**Lead:** Vladimir Darak (darak@vielendark.com)

### One-liner
Tells your CRM team exactly what to do next.

### What They Built
A decision intelligence agent that acts like a senior CRM strategist. Analyses campaigns, journeys, segments, and performance signals via Marketing and Analytics MCPs, then recommends what the team should prioritise next — with reasoning. Shipped with a live Vue.js web interface.

### Why It Scored High
Highest Demo score (4.8) — the live web interface made this tangible and immediately usable. The concept of an always-on strategic advisor resonated strongly with judges.
